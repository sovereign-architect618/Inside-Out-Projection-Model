## 2. Clarification A — one multilevel inverse relationship

The author affirmed that the following are all expressions of the same underlying operation:

1. opposite orientation or handedness;
2. counter-rotation between nested dimensional levels;
3. inverse winding across Clifford-torus cycles;
4. return complementing forward projection;
5. alternating inward and outward recursion; and
6. observational inversion under dimensional projection.

The central clarification is therefore:

> **Inverse rotation is one invariant generative relationship expressed simultaneously across geometry, topology, dimensional nesting, information flow, recursive direction, and observation.**

The return phase does not undo the forward projection. It transforms and reintegrates what occurred, updates the larger structure, and supplies changed conditions for the next projection.

## 3. Clarification B — persistence and becoming

The author clarified that coherence and entropy are not intended to reach exact equilibrium and that maximum coherence or complete elimination of entropy would be detrimental.

The accepted verbal relation is:

> **Enough structure to persist; enough variation to become.**

This identifies coherence and entropy as complementary requirements of viable recursion. Coherence without sufficient entropy-driven variation becomes rigidity; entropy without sufficient coherence becomes dissolution.

## 4. Why earlier summaries remain preserved

The earlier cohesive summaries accurately recorded the recovered local processing architecture and the components then explicitly connected. They did not adequately represent the complete spatial ontology or recognize the six inverse relationships as simultaneous expressions of one invariant principle.

Their omission does not mean the author abandoned the toroidal web, composite geometry, inverse rotation, or dynamic coherence–entropy relation. The recovery record shows those ideas elsewhere in the source history. The earlier summaries should therefore remain as pre-integration snapshots rather than being silently overwritten.

## 10. Author review resolutions — 2026-10-06

1. **Composite geometry:** the stellated octahedron and tesseract are the shapes the author understands to be present. Their exact construction cannot be specified more narrowly until candidate drawings are produced and compared with that understanding.
2. **Tilt/offset:** the two words originally described the same perceived relation. Formal geometry may later distinguish rotational tilt from translational offset if both are required by the confirmed construction.
3. **Selective inversion:** some recursions may preserve handedness while inverting another component. The mechanism should therefore allow identity action on some components and inverse/complementary action on others.
4. **Infinity:** recursive dimensional nesting is bi-infinite in principle. A one-direction-only infinity would contradict the intended logic.
5. **Clifford-torus roles:** $T_C$ may be carrier, cross-section, and repeating cell, with the applicable description determined by the level of analysis.
6. **Entropy terminology:** entropy remains the core term. “Entropy-like variation” may be used only as a technical qualifier when distinguishing an untyped cross-domain role from a domain-specific entropy measure; it must not replace or weaken the author's assertion.
7. **Persistence and becoming:** the principle is universal, while smaller sub-branches may instantiate and test it through different variables and mechanisms.
8. **Publication:** the author is ready for a public version that is substantially more consistent with the complete spatial and structural vision. Exact geometric drawings may follow as a later reviewed addition rather than delaying the textual architecture.
