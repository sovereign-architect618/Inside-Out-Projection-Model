## Two central organizing principles

### Multilevel inverse relation

Inverse rotation is one invariant generative relationship expressed across geometry, topology, dimensional nesting, information flow, recursive direction, and observation. Its manifestations include handedness, nested counter-rotation, complementary Clifford-torus winding, inverse return flow, inward/outward recursion, and observational inversion under dimensional projection.

A particular recursion may preserve one component while inverting another. The return phase does not merely rewind projection: it transforms and reintegrates local consequences, updates the larger relational structure, and establishes changed conditions for renewed projection.

### Persistence and becoming

> **Enough structure to persist; enough variation to become.**

Coherence supplies continuity, organization, identity, and memory. Entropy supplies variation, differentiation, movement, and the possibility of reorganization. Their viable relation is neither exact equilibrium nor maximization of coherence. Too little coherence produces dissolution; too little entropy-driven variation produces rigidity. The principle is universal within the architecture and may be instantiated through different variables and mechanisms at different scales.

## Status and provenance

This repository is a research and reconstruction workspace, not yet a final engineering handoff or canonical replacement release.

- The spatial vision, inside-out relation, toroidal recursion, nested dimensions, composite stellated-octahedron/tesseract geometry, coherence–entropy relation, and central conceptual corrections are author-originated.
- Typed operators, viability-set equations, tuple notation, diagrams, and other mathematical packaging are labeled as AI formalization or synthesis drafts where applicable.
- Omission from a later document is not treated as abandonment.
- Conflicting or unresolved versions are preserved rather than silently repaired.
- Mathematical candidates are evaluated by whether they realize the recovered architecture; they do not define the architecture merely because an equation exists.
